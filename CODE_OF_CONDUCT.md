# Code of conduct

Nibbler is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous community.

Contributors must communicate professionally, assume good faith, welcome people of all
backgrounds and experience levels, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy,
and stop behavior when asked. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary
to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. For
security vulnerabilities, use [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead. Maintainers will
protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible and will not tolerate
retaliation.

This policy applies to repository activity and to public interactions where someone is
representing Nibbler.
